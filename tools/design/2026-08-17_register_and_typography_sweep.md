# Register and typography sweep, and the audit of it

2026-08-17. Package at 0.2.2, built, checked `0E/0W/1N`, installed.
778 tests, 0 failures, 0 skips, 0 lints.

## Why the sweep happened

The maintainer reported seeing `--` in rendered output and asked for the
package and its two public documents to be brought to a formal register for a
general reader, constructive rather than defensive, with weaknesses stated
alongside options for addressing them.

## The defect behind the double hyphen

The construction ` -- ` is not uniformly wrong. It renders as a proper en dash
through pandoc and through R's Rd renderer, which covers the vignettes and the
help pages. It displays as two literal hyphen characters wherever nothing
processes typographic markup, which covers hand-written HTML and any markdown a
reader meets on a repository page.

The exposure was therefore concentrated where it was least visible to a
developer working inside the package.

| Surface | Renders as | Reader sees |
|---|---|---|
| Vignettes, via pandoc | en dash | correct |
| Help pages, via Rd | en dash | correct |
| `README.md`, `NEWS.md` on a repository page | unprocessed | two hyphens |
| Hand-written HTML | unprocessed | two hyphens |

The decision taken was to remove the construction rather than substitute a
different dash character. That fixes the display, removes the dependence on
which renderer happens to process a file, and matches the pending charter draft
that treats dash asides as a prose tell. Commas, separate sentences and
parentheses replaced them throughout.

## What was deliberately not changed

`tools/oracle/parity/matlab/extracted/` holds 33 generated byte-exact slices of the
original authors' published MATLAB, carrying 54 instances of the construction.
These were protected explicitly. A checker re-reads them against the upstream
source and fails on one byte of drift, and the verification argument of the
whole package rests on their being unmodified. They are gitignored because they
are reproducible rather than authored.

Verified after the sweep: 2,159 verbatim lines preserved, 54 instances intact,
and `check_extraction()` returns 33 records, 33 ok, 0 drifted.

Six further instances survive in string literals that the harness writes into
generated files, and one in a comment whose subject is the two-hyphen character
itself. Changing those would alter generated output or destroy the meaning.

## Defects found that were not typography

The sweep surfaced five substantive problems.

1. **A stale claim in a shipped file.** `inst/extdata/oracle-manifest.yml` said
   the causal influence range was "not yet implemented", false since 0.2.0,
   with a fixture 47 lines below grading exactly that.

2. **A wrong direction, caught before it shipped.** While writing a route
   forward for the censored-range limitation, the intuitive reading of `margin`
   turned out to be backwards. A larger margin requires more of the record to
   remain unused, so it produces more censoring, not less. Measured: 0 times
   censored at `margin = 0.001`, all 301 at `0.05` and above. The only route is
   a longer record, which is what the article now says.

3. **A generator that would have reverted its own fix.** `README.md` is built
   from `README.Rmd`. Editing the output alone would have been undone by the
   next `build_readme()`.

4. **Two documented strings out of step with the code.** The parity report
   quoted verdict strings the harness emits, one of which had changed. Both are
   now aligned at source.

5. **A duplicated line in `aci_cir()`'s `@returns`**, which had been rendering
   into the help page, and a duplicated comment paragraph.

## The audit

A separate reviewer audited the whole package and both documents afterwards.
Its headline finding was that the figure "2,159 verbatim reference lines" was
wrong and should read 2,075, in four places including both public documents.

**That finding is incorrect, and was not acted on.** Three independent
measurements give 2,159: the 37 declared ranges in the manifest sum to 2,159;
the 33 generated extracts contain exactly 2,159 lines between their VERBATIM
markers; and `check_extraction()` independently reports 2,159. Acting on the
report would have replaced a correct number with a wrong one.

The episode is recorded because it is the same lesson the package already
carries. A review is a claim, and a claim is checked rather than believed.

The audit's other findings held up and were fixed.

| Finding | Status |
|---|---|
| Manifest header at `package_version: 0.1.0`, package at 0.2.2 | corrected |
| Manifest platform `x86_64`, machine is `arm64` | corrected |
| Manifest naming 2 enforcing tests where there are 7 | corrected |
| `NEWS.md` contradicting itself on what a censored time returns | corrected |
| An informal metaphor surviving in the parity template | removed at source |
| Four em dashes in `cran-comments.md` | removed |
| Register survivors in `CONTRIBUTING` and `PARITY_REPORT` | removed |
| Commit count stated as 35, actual 41 | corrected |
| Two figures both stated as "26" under different counting rules | rule stated |

Two of these matter more than their size suggests. The manifest ships inside
the package and opens with a provenance claim, so a stale version and a wrong
architecture sit where a reviewer checking provenance looks first. The
`NEWS.md` contradiction is the failure the package's own retired-claims guard
exists to prevent; it did not catch it, because that guard checks a list of
specific known-retired phrases rather than scanning for internal
inconsistency.

## A structural improvement

Inside its incorrect finding, the audit made a fair observation. The parity
page stated that its content could not drift because it was pulled at render
time, while the headline statistics above that content were typed into the
template. The figures were right, but the mechanism was weaker than the claim.

Those statistics are now computed at render time from the manifest and the gate
reports. A stale card is impossible rather than merely unlikely, and the page's
claim about itself is now true of the whole page.

## Open items, and what was done about them

Three of the four were closed the same day. Test counts moved 777 to 778.

1. **The retired-claims guard has a known limit. Addressed.** It matches a list
   of specific phrases and cannot detect a new internal contradiction, which is
   how the `NEWS.md` inconsistency survived. Extending it into a general
   consistency checker would be a different and larger instrument. The cheap
   step was taken instead: both claims retired in this cycle are now entries in
   the list, so neither can return. The entries were verified by negative
   control rather than by their passing, since a guard entry that never fires
   proves nothing. Reintroducing the manifest claim makes the guard fail, and
   restoring the file makes it pass.

3. **Em dashes in three harness headers. Addressed.** Nine instances in
   `aci_oracle_cross.m`, `aci_oracle_dyad.m` and `validate_core.R`, now zero
   across `tools/oracle/` outside the generated extracts.

4. **The integrator fixture had no provenance record. Addressed.**
   `tests/testthat/fixtures/dyad_true_path_head.csv` sits under `tests/` rather
   than `inst/extdata`, so the manifest never reached it, and the promise of a
   record for each fixture stopped short. Its bytes are now pinned in the test
   that consumes it, using `tools::md5sum()` to match the manifest's own idiom,
   which avoids `tools::sha256sum()` because that arrived in R 4.5.0 and this
   package's declared floor is 4.1.0.

The remaining item was assessed and deliberately left.

2. **About 70 prose colons remain in test comments.** The "claim, then
   elaboration" pattern is the dominant explanatory idiom there. Converting
   them would be a larger rewrite of load-carrying developer documentation than
   the dash work itself, with a real regression risk and little reader benefit,
   since these comments are not read by a general audience. Left deliberately.

3. **Three oracle harness files carry pre-existing em dashes in their headers.**
   They are outside any rendering path today. If those files ever feed a
   rendered document they will need the same treatment.

4. **The integrator fixture has no manifest entry.**
   `tests/testthat/fixtures/dyad_true_path_head.csv` is the only oracle-bearing
   fixture without a provenance record and hash. Its integrity is covered
   indirectly, since a test ties its observed column to the hashed packaged
   signal, but the manifest promises a record for each fixture and does not
   reach this one.
