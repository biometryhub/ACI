# aciR: Assimilative Causal Inference for Conditional Gaussian Nonlinear Systems

The aciR package implements assimilative causal inference (ACI) for
conditional Gaussian nonlinear systems (CGNS). A CGNS pairs an observed
signal with an unobserved component whose conditional statistics, given
the observed path, are Gaussian and available in closed form.

## Details

Most analyses need three calls.
[`aci_dyad_model()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_dyad_model.md)
or
[`aci_cgns_model()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_cgns_model.md)
builds an `aci_model`,
[`aci_simulate()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_simulate.md)
draws a realisation of it, and
[`aci()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci.md) runs the
whole workflow on an observed signal and returns an `aci` object with
[`summary.aci()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/summary.aci.md),
[`plot.aci()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/plot.aci.md)
and
[`as.data.frame.aci()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/as.data.frame.aci.md)
methods.

Beneath that sits the numerical core, comprising the forward filter
([`aci_filter()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_filter.md)),
the backward smoother
([`aci_smoother()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_smoother.md))
and the relative-entropy causal-information metric
([`aci_metric()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_metric.md)).
The three are written against a general CGNS components list rather than
a specific model, so they apply to any conditional Gaussian system.
[aci_components](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_components.md)
documents that schema and
[`aci_dyad_components()`](https://biometryhub.github.io/ACI/reference/aci_dyad_components.md)
is its worked example.

The numerical core is graded against fixtures generated by an
independent MATLAB harness reproducing the deterministic core of the
reference implementation. The *Validation and the independent oracle*
vignette records the design, the provenance and the observed agreement;
the *Assumptions and interpretation* vignette states what the method
assumes and what an ACI peak does and does not support.

The method reimplemented here is due to Andreou, Chen and Bollt (2026).
